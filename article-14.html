Google Find Hub rolls out ‘Notify when left behind’ tracker alerts on Android

9to5Google · Thu 05 Nov 21:30

As previewed in October , Google has added “Notify when left behind” alerts for Find Hub tags and trackers on Android.

To enable, open the Find Hub app and choose a tag. In the top-right corner of the bottom sheet, select the settings gear icon.

There is a new “Notify when left behind” section to “Receive a notification when this tag is separated from your phone.” By default, there’s an exception for the Home address you’ve stored in your Google Account (Maps).
