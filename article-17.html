Pixel 11 and Pixel 10 at up to $350 off are great phone deals that will only be harder to find in RAMageddon

9to5Google · Thu 05 Nov 19:05

As RAMageddon’s impact squeezes smartphones more and more, great deals on a new smartphone are going to get harder to find. Right now, Google’s Pixel 11 series and the Pixel 10 are $200 off, and that’s absolutely worth a look.

With smartphone prices going up and no end to RAMageddon in sight , buying a new smartphone, frankly, sucks right now. The latest generations are expensive and not seeing as many discounts, while older phones are either out of stock or discounted back to their original prices after price hikes.

Finding a good deal, that’s going to get harder.

Right now, one of the few actually good deals on a smartphone can be found across Google’s Pixel lineup. The best of the bunch is $200 off of a Pixel 10, with the 128GB model down to $599 brand new.
