Keanu Reeves Officially Returning for ‘John Wick’ Animated Film

Gizmodo · Thu 05 Nov 22:35

Keanu Reeves is returning as John Wick , but maybe not exactly as you expected. Lionsgate just greenlit a previously teased John Wick animated prequel, which Reeves will produce and lend his voice to. The film will finally reveal to fans the “Impossible Task” Wick had to complete to detach himself from the High Table and its world of assassins: kill every single one of his rivals in a single night.
