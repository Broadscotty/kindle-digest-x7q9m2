9to5Google · Thu 05 Nov 23:41

Just over a month after the launch, Google is rolling out the “Googlebook November update” with “many bug fixes, stability improvements, and firmware updates.”

There are updates across seven areas. Google first lists changes that benefit all users before detailing device-specific improvements. The HP (eight) and Dell XPS (nine) models see the most updates.
