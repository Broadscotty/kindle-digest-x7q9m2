Samsung brings back free doubled storage discount on Galaxy Z Fold 8

9to5Google · Thu 05 Nov 20:30

Samsung is running its much-loved “double the storage” promo on its Galaxy Z Fold 8 right now.

A common pre-order perk over the years on Samsung Galaxy devices has been doubled storage at no added cost. Basically, get the next tier up at the cost of the base model. Samsung has brought this back occasionally after pre-orders end, but usually with a catch or two.

That’s what we’re seeing right now on the Galaxy Z Fold 8.

You can currently get the Galaxy Z Fold 8 with “double the storage on select colors” from Samsung.com right now. That limited color is “Lavender,” but the other catch is that it also only applies to the upper 1TB model.
